After logging into your account, you'll see your dashboard. It shows widgets customized for your profile and privileges.

In the next section, you will learn about the HSU (Health Service User) journey and the different workflows in OpenMRS.

### Setting the language of your account

To change the language, click on your name and wait for the "My Account" button to appear.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (447).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>How to find my Account</p></figcaption></figure>

Then click on the button "My Languages" and select the language you need.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (448).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>My Languages</p></figcaption></figure>
